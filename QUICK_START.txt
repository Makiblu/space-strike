🚀 HOW TO PLAY:

1. Open Google Chrome
2. Press Ctrl+O (or File > Open)
3. Navigate to: C:\Users\User\OneDrive\Desktop\SPACE FIGHTER
4. Select: index.html
5. Click Open

   OR

1. Open File Explorer
2. Go to: C:\Users\User\OneDrive\Desktop\SPACE FIGHTER
3. Right-click index.html
4. Select "Open with" > Google Chrome

🎮 CONTROLS:

WASD / Arrow Keys - Move
SPACE - Shoot
X - Fire Missile
C - Special Ability
ESC - Pause/Resume

📋 FIRST TIME SETUP:

1. Wait for loading screen (2-3 seconds)
2. Enter a username (any name)
3. Click "NEW PROFILE"
4. You're in the Main Menu!

🎯 WHAT TO TRY:

→ QUICK PLAY: Jump straight into arcade action
→ FIGHTER HANGAR: Select your ship (FALCON-X unlocked)
→ RECRUIT FIGHTERS: Spend 1000 starting coins to unlock more
→ ADVENTURE MODE: Play through 10 chapters, 50 levels
→ BOT ARENA: Fight AI opponents
→ INVENTORY: Use your 3 starting health potions
→ UPGRADES: Improve your stats with coins

💾 YOUR PROGRESS IS SAVED AUTOMATICALLY
   (Local to this browser - don't clear browser data!)
